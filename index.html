步步为营

Quoridor

Walk a pawn to the far rank. Pinch, or close your hand, to pick up your pawn or a fence from your stack. Open your palm to set it down. Tilt your palm to turn a fence.

A fence covers two squares. It may touch another at the end. It may not cross one, and it may not seal either pawn off from its goal.

The camera picture stays on this device. Mouse: drag and let go. Right-click or R turns a fence. Space starts, M mutes.
